**APPALACHIAN VALLEY MEDICAL CENTER**

Acceptable Use Policy

*AVMC Information Technology & Security*

| **Policy Owner** | AVMC Information Technology and Security                                                              |
|------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Applies To**   | Workforce members, contractors, students, vendors, volunteers, and other authorized users             |
| **Review Cycle** | At least annually and after significant technology, operational, legal, or cybersecurity-risk changes |

# 1. Purpose

Appalachian Valley Medical Center’s (AVMC) Acceptable Use Policy
establishes requirements for the safe, responsible, and authorized use
of AVMC systems, networks, devices, applications, and data.

AVMC relies on technology for patient care, clinical operations, imaging
and laboratory services, pharmacy operations, administration, building
management, physical security, and other hospital functions. Misuse of
these resources can expose AVMC to cybersecurity incidents, unauthorized
disclosure of sensitive information, service disruptions, malware,
ransomware, and other operational risks.

All users must use AVMC technology to support organizational operations
while protecting the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of
AVMC systems and information.

# 2. Scope

This policy applies to anyone who accesses technology resources owned or
managed by AVMC. These resources include workstations, laptops, servers,
network equipment, clinical systems, medical and IoT devices, wireless
networks, applications, email, internet access, removable media,
building-management and physical-security systems, and any other devices
connected to AVMC networks.

This policy applies whether access is made from an AVMC-owned device, an
authorized personal device, or an approved remote location. Devices used
by guests and patients on AVMC’s guest wireless network must comply with
AVMC network restrictions and may not access internal AVMC systems.

# 3. Acceptable Use Requirements

AVMC technology resources shall be used primarily for authorized
healthcare, administrative, operational, educational, and other
legitimate organizational purposes. Users may access only the systems,
applications, network segments, files, and information required for
their assigned duties. Technical access to a resource does not
constitute authorization to use it.

Users must safeguard their credentials and may not share passwords or
accounts. They are responsible for all activity conducted through their
assigned accounts. When appropriate, users shall lock or sign out of
unattended AVMC workstations and devices and take reasonable steps to
prevent unauthorized access to or viewing of AVMC information. Sensitive
organizational, patient, employee, and operational information shall be
handled only through authorized AVMC systems and services.

# 4. Prohibited Activities

- Attempt to circumvent AVMC security controls, including access-control
  lists, network segmentation, authentication requirements, and
  monitoring systems.

- Access patient, clinical, administrative, security, or operational
  information without an authorized business or clinical purpose.

- Install unauthorized software, applications, network equipment,
  wireless access points, or devices on AVMC systems or networks.

- Disable or disrupt endpoint protection, logging, authentication,
  security monitoring, or other AVMC security controls.

- Introduce malicious software or intentionally engage in activities
  that could disrupt AVMC systems or services.

- Perform unauthorized scanning, exploitation, password attacks,
  interception, or other security testing.

- Connect unauthorized removable media or external storage to AVMC
  systems.

- Share confidential AVMC information through unauthorized personal
  email, cloud storage, messaging, social media, or file-sharing
  services.

- Use another person’s account or allow anyone else to use assigned
  credentials.

- Use AVMC technology for illegal, abusive, fraudulent, or materially
  disruptive purposes.

Limited incidental personal use may be allowed if it does not interfere
with work duties, consume excessive resources, violate AVMC policy,
create cybersecurity risks, or involve sensitive AVMC information.

# 5. Email, Internet, and Phishing Safety

Users must exercise caution when opening email attachments, clicking
links, downloading files, or responding to unexpected requests for
information. They must promptly report suspicious messages, unexpected
authentication requests, unusual system behavior, suspected malware, and
potential phishing attempts through AVMC’s incident-reporting process.

Users must not knowingly open or run suspicious attachments or bypass
browser, email, or endpoint-security warnings without authorization from
AVMC IT/Security. A compromised account or endpoint can give an attacker
an initial foothold to spread ransomware or other malicious activity.

# 6. Network and Wireless Use

Users shall connect AVMC devices only to network resources authorized
for their roles and device types. AVMC maintains logically separated
network zones for distinct operational purposes, including clinical
systems, medical IoT, servers, IT management, guest access, and
Facilities/BMS systems. Users shall not attempt to bypass these
boundaries.

Guest and personal devices shall use the designated guest network when
applicable and may access protected AVMC internal resources only with
specific authorization. Medical IoT and Facilities/BMS devices shall
communicate only with the systems and services necessary for their
intended functions.

# 7. Protection of Sensitive Information

AVMC information shall be accessed, stored, transmitted, or disclosed
only as required for authorized duties. Users must take reasonable steps
to prevent unauthorized individuals from viewing sensitive information
on screens, printed documents, or other media.

Sensitive AVMC information shall not be copied to personal devices,
personal cloud-storage accounts, unauthorized removable media, or other
unapproved locations. Any suspected loss, exposure, alteration, or
unauthorized disclosure of AVMC information must be reported
immediately.

# 8. Security Monitoring and Privacy Expectations

AVMC may monitor, log, and review activity on its systems and networks
for cybersecurity, operations, compliance, troubleshooting, and incident
response. Users should not expect activity on AVMC-owned or managed
technology to be private from authorized monitoring. Security logs and
monitoring data must be protected from unauthorized access or
alteration.

# 9. Security Incident Reporting

Users must promptly report suspected cybersecurity incidents to AVMC
IT/Security, including suspicious emails, ransomware messages,
unexpected file changes, lost or stolen devices, unauthorized access,
malware alerts, unusual system behavior, exposed credentials, and
suspected disclosures of sensitive information.

Unless directed by authorized incident-response personnel, users shall
not independently investigate, delete evidence, negotiate with
attackers, or attempt remediation outside established AVMC procedures.

# 10. Responsibilities

- All users: follow this policy, safeguard credentials and devices, and
  promptly report suspected security incidents.

- Managers and department leaders: ensure personnel understand
  acceptable-use requirements and have access appropriate to their
  duties.

- AVMC IT/Security personnel: implement and maintain safeguards, monitor
  systems, investigate suspected violations, and support incident
  response.

- AVMC leadership: approve cybersecurity policy, support enforcement,
  and provide resources to manage cybersecurity risk.

Policy violations may result in restricted or revoked access and, when
appropriate, additional administrative, contractual, or legal action.
