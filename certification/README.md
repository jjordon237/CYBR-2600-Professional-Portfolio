## Readiness & Study Approach

My preparation focuses on connecting exam objectives with hands-on work and documenting areas that need improvement. I distinguish documented completion records from self-reported assessment results. The reported 60/60 Cloud Foundations practice score is not represented as an official certification result.

1. Review the current official exam objectives and identify knowledge gaps.
2. Complete focused learning and hands-on exercises relevant to those gaps.
3. Record the assessment date, provider, result, and areas for further study when available.
4. Reassess readiness before scheduling an examination.
5. Add an earned certification only after successful completion and verification.
